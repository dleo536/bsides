# b.sides Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

This privacy policy describes how b.sides collects, uses, stores, and deletes data when you use the b.sides mobile app.

## Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to the b.sides iOS and Android app, including account creation, profile features, lists, reviews, moderation tools, and support requests sent through the published support channels.

## Data b.sides collects

### 1. Account and contact information

b.sides collects:

- Email address
- Username
- Internal account identifiers such as Firebase Authentication UID and backend user ID

These details are used to create your account, authenticate you, and keep your profile, reviews, and lists attached to the correct user.

### 2. Profile information

b.sides may store:

- Profile photo
- Display name
- Bio
- Location
- Website URL
- Favorite genres and favorite artists

This information is used to render your profile and personalize your presence in the app.

### 3. User content

b.sides stores the content you create in the app, including:

- Album reviews
- Ratings
- Review headlines and body text
- Track notes and tags
- Album lists and list descriptions
- Reports you submit about objectionable content

This information is used to power the social and moderation features of the app.

### 4. Safety and moderation data

To operate user-generated content safely, b.sides may store:

- Content report details
- Block relationships between accounts
- Moderation status and review notes
- Timestamps associated with reports and account actions

This information is used to investigate reports, reduce abuse, and enforce platform rules.

### 5. Service and infrastructure data

b.sides uses third-party infrastructure such as Firebase Authentication, Firebase Storage, and backend services hosted on Google Cloud. Those providers may process limited technical information such as request logs, IP addresses, and service-level diagnostics as part of operating the service.

## How b.sides uses data

b.sides uses collected data to:

- Create and secure accounts
- Display profiles, lists, and reviews
- Upload and serve profile photos
- Operate follows, blocks, and report flows
- Moderate objectionable content and abusive users
- Respond to support, privacy, and safety issues
- Maintain, debug, and protect the service

## Third parties and external services

b.sides relies on third-party services to operate parts of the app:

- Firebase Authentication for sign-in
- Firebase Storage for profile images
- Google Cloud-hosted backend services for app data
- Spotify and MusicBrainz for music metadata requests

b.sides does not sell your personal information.

## Data retention

b.sides keeps account data while your account is active or while needed to operate the service, investigate abuse, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

Moderation records and limited service logs may be retained longer where reasonably necessary for safety, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

## Your choices

### Delete your account

You can request account deletion inside the app from:

`Profile > overflow menu > Delete account`

When you delete your account, b.sides removes active account access and deletes your stored profile, lists, reviews, and profile image from active systems. Limited backup copies may persist for a short period before being overwritten or removed.

### Report or block

You can:

- Report profiles, reviews, and lists in-app
- Block user accounts from their profile menu

These controls are provided to help reduce abusive or objectionable content.
